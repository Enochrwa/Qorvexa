# Ideas backlog

A parking lot for ideas. Nothing here is committed. Promote an idea to the [backlog](../planning/backlog.md) once it has a user problem, a requirement ID and a size.

## Discovery
- **Mood and moment picker:** "I have 30 minutes", "feel-good", "cry a little" mapped to tagged collections.
- **Because-you-watched explanations** with a one-tap "not interested" that retrains rows.
- **Cast and crew pages** with filmography.
- **Language-first browse:** filter by spoken language and available subtitles.
- **Weekly discovery challenge:** watch a title outside your usual genres, earn a badge (opt-in).
- **Editor's picks** with short written notes by the owner.

## Player
- **Chapter thumbnails and scene search**.
- **Audio-only mode** for drama listening on low data.
- **Data-saver mode** with lower start quality and no preview autoplay.
- **Replay last 15 seconds** button and subtitle "what did they say?" quick rewind with captions on.
- **Sleep timer** and "stop after this episode".
- **Frame-accurate scrubbing** for admins when marking intro/recap.

## Series experience
- **Recap generator** from owner-written summaries (spoiler toggle).
- **Release calendar** with "remind me" per episode.
- **Binge mode** that skips recap and credits automatically.
- **Character/relationship guide** per series (owner content).

## Social
- **Watch parties** with synchronized playback and chat.
- **Share a moment:** timestamp link with a preview card.
- **Profiles for families** with kids mode and PIN-protected maturity.

## Growth and monetization (future)
- Mobile money payments and rentals.
- Ad-supported tier with simple pre-roll (VAST) later.
- Referral rewards (free rental).
- Multi-tenant white-label for other content owners.

## Platform
- **Open Graph prerender** for title pages so shared links show posters.
- **Edge-cached catalog** using a CDN worker.
- **AI helpers:** semantic search, auto-generated chapters and summaries, subtitle translation (cost and quality review first).
- **Offline library** via Shaka Player where supported.
- **Casting** (Chromecast) and TV-optimized UI.
- **Accessibility extras:** audio description tracks, dyslexia-friendly subtitle font.

## Admin
- **Content calendar** and bulk scheduling.
- **Quality dashboard:** failed uploads, slow-start titles, devices with errors.
- **Cost dashboard:** delivered minutes, stored minutes, projected bill, per-title cost.
- **Auto-QC:** loudness and black-frame detection after processing.
