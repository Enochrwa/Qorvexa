# Legal and compliance

> This is an engineering checklist, not legal advice. Have the customer confirm requirements with a qualified lawyer for their jurisdiction(s).

## Content rights

- Only upload content the customer owns or is licensed to distribute; record the rights basis per title (`rights_note`, `licensed_until`, territories).
- Support availability windows and territory restrictions where licenses require them.
- Provide a takedown process: a public contact, a report button, a documented response time, and an audit trail.

## Data protection

- Rwanda has a personal data protection law (Law No. 058/2021); if serving EU users, GDPR applies. Verify obligations with counsel.
- Practices: collect minimum data, state purposes, provide access/export and deletion, record consent for notifications, keep processors (hosts, email, analytics) listed.
- Children: age ratings and maturity limits; avoid collecting data from children without a lawful basis.

## Documents to publish before the pilot

- Terms of Service
- Privacy Policy (what, why, how long, processors, rights, contact)
- Cookie notice (only if non-essential cookies are used)
- Content policy and takedown procedure
- Contact page

## Third-party terms

Check each provider's acceptable-use and commercial terms (video hosting, free-plan restrictions, branding requirements such as the Mux badge on the free plan, CDN rules about video on free plans).

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA; provide captions for content where available and let the owner upload subtitle tracks.

## Records to keep

Rights documentation per title, takedown requests and outcomes, consent records, data deletion requests, security incidents.
